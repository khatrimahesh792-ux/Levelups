## How will you level up?

🎯Focus on one direction

Put most of your effort into one direction at a time. You can switch anytime.

🌟All directions together

Work on several directions in the same day. Working on 3 or more in one day gives +10% bonus XP.

## How XP works

| 1 minute of timed work | 1 XP |
| --- | --- |
| Daily cap per direction | 150 XP |
| Max per session | 180 min |
| Minimum session | 5 min |
